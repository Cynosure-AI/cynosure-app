# Bergbuch (eigene App)

Bergbuch is my side project: a hiking and climbing logbook app.

## Tech
- Flutter for iOS and Android
- Supabase (Postgres, Auth, Storage) on the Pro plan, 25 $ per month
- Maps via MapLibre with OpenTopoMap tiles

## Status (September 2026)
- Closed beta with 212 testers
- Most requested feature: offline maps
- Crash rate below 0.4 % since version 0.9.3

## Business idea
- Free core app, "Bergbuch Pro" for 2,99 € per month with offline maps and GPX export
- Break-even at about 40 paying users (Supabase plus Apple developer fee)

## Next steps
1. Offline map tiles prototype
2. Privacy policy update for location data
3. Public launch planned for spring 2027
